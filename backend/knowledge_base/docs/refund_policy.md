# Refund Policy

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## Approval Thresholds
- Refunds of $100 or less: automatically approved by the agent.
- Refunds over $100: require human approval before processing.

## Processing Time
- Credit card refunds: 5-10 business days.
- Store credit: instant.

## Exceptions
- Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged.
- Refunds are issued in the same currency as the original purchase.

## Multi-Tenant Note
Each tenant has its own order history. An agent may only view and refund orders belonging to the tenant it is serving.
